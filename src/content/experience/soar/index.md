SOAR, the University of Calgary's Student Organization for Aerospace Research, is where I got the widest education of my degree. Working on the Ouroboros, Aegis and Athena hybrid rockets, I built up real experience in mechanical design, manufacturing, embedded software, electronics and systems design, often on the same piece of hardware in the same week.

It's also where I found my calling. Seeing hardware we designed, built and tested ourselves leave the rail turned an interest in aerospace into a burning desire to work on cutting-edge technology, in a fast-paced environment, alongside people who build things that fly.
