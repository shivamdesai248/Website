## Overview

Alongside the software, I led the mechanical design of how SOAR's avionics are integrated into the rocket: the structures that carry the electronics, the batteries that power them, and the ground equipment used to weigh the rocket.

## Avionics bays

- Led the mechanical design of the avionics integration systems for **Ouroboros**, **Aegis** and **Athena**.
- Designed the **Ouroboros avionics bay** in SolidWorks to fit the onboard electronics within radio-transparency and size constraints, and built it with laser cutting, waterjet cutting and FDM printing.
- Designed a **modular, metal additively manufactured avionics bay** for Aegis using design-for-additive-manufacturing principles.
- Checked the structures with hand calculations and FEA in **ANSYS Mechanical** and SolidWorks.

## Rocket Weigh System

- Redesigned the **Rocket Weigh System** for Ouroboros, and developed a new version for Aegis, to fix load-cell drift and improve measurement accuracy.

## Power

- Designed **21700 and 18650 Li-ion flight battery packs** for Ouroboros and Aegis.

## Reviews

- Presented designs at technical design reviews and gave critical feedback in others.
